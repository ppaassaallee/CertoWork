# Certo Work Strategy

Strategy is an integrated execution layer, not a separate task system.

## Operating model

1. **Objective:** a memorable, qualitative direction for one cycle. Objectives are committed, aspirational, or learning-oriented.
2. **Outcome measures:** two to five quantitative results with a start, current value, target, unit, and target date. Objective progress is calculated from these measures.
3. **Lead measures:** predictive, influenceable actions reviewed weekly. A measure can be manual or linked to an existing Project, Epic, or PBI.
4. **Scoreboard:** a compact view of progress, health, outcome measures, lead measures, owner, date, and weekly pulse.
5. **Weekly pulse:** one self-authored commitment per objective. Every check-in is retained as a strategy record.

Projects and work items remain canonical in their existing collections. Linking them to Strategy does not copy or flatten them. Completion of linked work can update a lead measure, but never substitutes for an outcome measure.

## Gems and rewards

- A Boost adds Gems to a Project or Epic wallet.
- Only workspace owners/admins or the named Project Manager/Sponsor can give a Boost for that project.
- Every Boost requires a reason that names the result or behavior worth repeating.
- Redemptions subtract Gems through a new ledger entry; balances are never overwritten.
- Workspace leaders create prizes with a Gem cost, stock, and fulfillment description.
- Redemption reduces stock and creates a requested fulfillment record.
- No negative awards, hidden deductions, or public-shaming leaderboard are used.

The same `strategic_goals`, `key_results`, and `strategic_initiatives` collections are retained for compatibility. `recordType` distinguishes weekly check-ins, Boosts, prizes, and redemptions additively.
