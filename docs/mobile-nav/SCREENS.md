## Copy rules
- Sentence case
- Verbs say what happens ("Create item", "Show 12 projects", "Approve")
- No "Submit", no "OK"
- Empty states say what to do ("No notes yet. Tap + to write one.")
- Never show a zero-count line as a sentence
